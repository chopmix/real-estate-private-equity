# Playbook: Industrial

Engine: `lease` (lease-by-lease). Read with `metrics-and-formulas.md` and `standards.md`.

## 1. What it is

| Subtype | Description | Main risk |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| Logistics / distribution | Large bulk and last-mile warehouses, clear height, dock doors | Supply waves, tenant concentration |
| Flex / light industrial | Small bays, office mix, many small tenants | Rollover volume, TI churn |
| Cold storage | Temperature-controlled, power heavy, specialized | Operator credit, obsolescence, power cost |
| Industrial outdoor storage (IOS) | Yard-based, little building, truck and trailer parking | Zoning, land value, tenant quality |
| Manufacturing / single-tenant | Built for one user | Residual value, see net-lease playbook |

## 2. How income is produced

- Lease by lease. Large buildings have one to few tenants. Flex has many.
- Structures: NNN is the norm for bulk and IOS. Flex is often NNN or modified gross.
- Annual fixed bumps are common. Check whether bumps are fixed or CPI-linked.
- Cold storage may use throughput or storage-plus-handling pricing, with minimum guarantees. Confirm contract form.
- IOS income depends on land use rights. Confirm entitlements.

## 3. Key metrics

| Metric | How to compute | Why it matters |
|--------|----------------|----------------|
| WALT | Remaining term weighted by rent | Core of value for bulk assets |
| Rollover by year | Rent expiring each year / total rent | Re-leasing cost and downtime |
| Mark to market | Market rent / in-place rent minus 1 | Rent resets drive growth |
| Net effective rent | PV of rent less free rent, TI and commissions per SF | Compares deals fairly |
| Tenant concentration | Largest tenant as share of rent | One exit breaks coverage |
| Clear height and dock ratio | Feet clear, doors per 10,000 SF | Functional fit |
| Coverage (IOS) | Building SF / land SF | Yard use and re-use options |
| Basis per SF | Price / SF vs replacement cost | Supply response limit |

## 4. Revenue drivers

- Contract rent and bumps.
- Rollover: renewal probability, downtime, free rent and market rent.
- Recoveries under NNN, with admin fee and caps.
- Expansion and contraction rights.
- Excess land or trailer parking income.
- Cold storage minimums and escalators tied to power cost.

## 5. Expense drivers

- Property tax. Model reassessment on sale where the jurisdiction reassesses.
- Insurance, including higher rates for cold storage and coastal or hail areas.
- Repairs: roofs, docks, paving and sprinklers.
- Utilities (landlord-paid common areas, power for cold storage).
- Management fee as a percent of EGI.
- Reserves below NOI for roof, paving, dock equipment and refrigeration plants.

## 6. Capital needs

- TI is light for bulk and heavier for flex and cold storage.
- Leasing commissions, new vs renewal.
- Roof replacement and paving.
- Dock, door and sprinkler upgrades to reach modern specifications.
- Refrigeration systems, panels and insulation for cold storage.
- Yard improvements for IOS: paving, fencing, lighting and drainage.

## 7. Debt and lender view

- Lenders: banks, life companies, CMBS, debt funds.
- Bulk with strong WALT often hits LTV or DSCR. Flex and IOS often hit debt yield.
- Lenders haircut single-tenant assets that lack credit or term.
- Cold storage and IOS often get lower proceeds due to specialized use.
- Rule of thumb, verify locally: lenders will size to in-place income and require reserves for near-term rollover.

## 8. Screening quick tests

| Test | Pass looks like |
|------|-----------------|
| WALT vs hold | Term supports the hold, or re-leasing risk is priced |
| Rent vs market | In-place rent at or below market |
| Basis vs replacement cost | Below cost to build, including land |
| Functional fit | Clear height, truck court, power and dock count meet current users |
| Location | Near labor, highways, ports or population |
| Tenant concentration | Credit supports a single-tenant risk |

## 9. Diligence additions

- Lease abstracts with options, purchase rights, termination, assignment and sublease terms.
- Review tenant financials and parent guarantees.
- Property condition report on roof, slab, sprinklers and electrical.
- Phase I environmental report, with Phase II where warranted. Check for hazardous material use by tenants.
- Truck circulation test and a turning radius check.
- Zoning compliance, especially for IOS and outdoor storage.
- For cold storage: refrigeration plant age, ammonia safety program, power contracts.

## 10. Red flags

- Single tenant with weak credit and short term.
- Building function behind current standards (low clear height, tight truck court, few docks).
- Recent supply surge near the asset.
- Tenant using hazardous materials with no environmental record.
- IOS with no permitted use or short land lease.
- Cold storage with old refrigeration and heavy power cost.
- Rent growth underwritten above what the lease bumps allow.
- Roof or paving at end of life with no reserve.

## 11. Value levers

- Early renewal and extension with existing tenants.
- Mark below-market rents to market at rollover.
- Subdivide large buildings into smaller bays, where demand supports it.
- Add dock doors, trailer parking or yard.
- Entitle and improve outdoor storage land.
- Fix recoveries and leakage.

## 12. Local intelligence

- Tenant news first: credit events, layoffs, closures, plant moves, consolidation of distribution networks.
- Government lease actions and port or freight policy changes.
- New supply: speculative construction, permits and land sales near the asset.
- Sublease space and tenant move-outs in the submarket.
- Utility and interconnection announcements.

## 13. Model inputs to capture

| Input | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Tenant, suite or bay, SF | One row per lease |
| Lease start, expiry, options | Include notice dates |
| Base rent per SF and bumps | Fixed or CPI |
| Lease structure | NNN, modified gross, gross |
| Market rent per SF | By building size and use |
| Renewal probability | Tenant-level |
| Downtime months | New vs renewal |
| Free rent months | New vs renewal |
| TI per SF | New vs renewal |
| Leasing commission | New vs renewal |
| Recoveries, admin fee, caps | Per SF |
| Opex lines | Tax, insurance, R&M, utilities, management |
| Tax reassessment | Year and value after sale |
| Reserves per SF | Roof, paving, docks, refrigeration |
| Yard or land income (IOS) | Acres and rate |

## 14. Site visit and management questions

- Drive the truck court. Check turning, queuing and parking on streets.
- Look at roof, slab cracks, dock equipment and sprinklers.
- Ask each tenant how long they plan to stay and what space they need next.
- Ask about power, water and expansion capacity.
- Ask what tenants store or make, and what they discharge.
- For cold storage: tour the plant and ask about maintenance and downtime.
