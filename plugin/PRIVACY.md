# Privacy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This plugin is a set of instructions and reference files for Claude. It has no servers, no connectors of its own, no analytics and no tracking. The author receives no data from your use of it.

## What it reads

Documents and data you give Claude, such as offering memoranda, rent rolls, financial statements, leases and loan documents. Some of these can contain personal data, for example tenant or resident names. The skills read that data only to complete the task you asked for.

## What it stores

Nothing. Files the skills produce, such as your buy box, deal log, workbooks and memos, are saved in your own Claude workspace or wherever you choose to keep them.

## What it sends

Nothing to the author or to any third party. When a skill does public research, it uses Claude's own web tools under your Claude settings.

## Retention

The plugin and its author retain nothing. How Anthropic handles your conversations and files is governed by your agreement with Anthropic.
